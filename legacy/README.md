Simple POS is a POS system that can run as a webapp.

Current scope:
-Create POS items with name & price
-Run customers orders, see what they order and what they need to pay
-Save orders to a complete order tally per sales period
-View total sales + revenue per sales period
-Save sales period into a xml file to keep in your spreadsheets

Simple POS is still in active development and must be seen as a barebones proof of concept :) Bugs may hide in the walls.
